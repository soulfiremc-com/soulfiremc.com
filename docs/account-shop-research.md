# Account shop research

Research date: 2026-11-01. This reference supports the five directory entries in `src/lib/accounts-data.tsx`.
It records public evidence for maintainers who update listings or assess integrations.

Catalog prices are snapshots before checkout fees. Published warranties describe the provider's policy, not a tested outcome.
Research included catalogs, product pages, policies, official social links, public API documentation, and affiliate terms.
No purchases, account registrations, affiliate applications, authenticated API calls, or messages to providers were made.

The directory lists permanent full-access accounts for all five shops.
Temporary Game Pass access, offline accounts, cape codes, and name-only offers do not establish a comparable full-access starting price.
Eldorado, G2G, and GameBoost therefore show **Varies by seller**.
Their entries omit a numeric price for sorting and structured data.

No new entry has a Soulfire affiliate, partner, official integration, or high-quality badge.
A public affiliate program does not establish a relationship with Soulfire.
Instant delivery applies only to FastAlts, whose public product and policy pages describe automatic stock delivery.
The other catalogs include external checkout or seller-dependent delivery.

## AltsGreat

### Catalog and pricing

- [Minecraft account catalog](https://altsgreat.com/categories/minecraft-accounts): full-access Java + Bedrock accounts, Hypixel ranks, capes, and established profiles.
- Advertised examples: Hypixel-unbanned $6.99, DonutSMP-unbanned $8.99, fresh Java + Bedrock $9.99, VIP $10.49, and MVP+ $25.99.
- [Hypixel-unbanned product](https://altsgreat.com/products/unbanned-fa): advertises permanent access and transfer to the buyer's email. DonutSMP status is not checked.
- Separate catalogs cover [Bedwars](https://altsgreat.com/categories/bedwars-accounts), [Skyblock](https://altsgreat.com/categories/skyblock-accounts), and [multiple capes](https://altsgreat.com/categories/multicape-accounts).

The public page data contains Eldorado payment links for multiple account products, including the Hypixel-unbanned offer.
These links contain AltsGreat referral identifiers. The directory links to AltsGreat's catalog without copying those identifiers.
Checkout prices, seller identity, stock, and payment terms need a separate check on the destination offer.
Catalog prices do not prove that the external checkout offers the same price.

### Payments, delivery, and warranty

The [extras page](https://altsgreat.com/policies/extras) lists card and cryptocurrency payments, with other options and fees at checkout.
It describes delivery as usually immediate or within minutes. It directs delayed orders and warranty claims to Discord.
Resale requires prior written permission.

The [terms](https://altsgreat.com/policies/terms-of-service) make warranties specific to each product.
The [about page](https://altsgreat.com/policies/who-are-we) advertises lifetime coverage for eligible products, without establishing universal coverage.
The [refund policy](https://altsgreat.com/policies/refund-policy) offers refunds for faulty products when support cannot fix or replace them.
It requests technical issue reports within 24 hours. Refund processing can take seven business days.

### Links and open questions

- Website: [altsgreat.com](https://altsgreat.com/).
- Reviews: [AltsGreat on Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/altsgreat.com). The profile includes adverse account-recovery reports, which are customer allegations.
- Omitted Discord: `https://discord.gg/mQwHJevaSs`. Discord's public invite endpoint returned an expired-invite response on the research date.
- Logo source: [official site icon](https://altsgreat.com/icon.png), saved unchanged as `public/accounts/altsgreat.png`.
- Start date: omitted. A Trustpilot claim date does not establish the shop's founding date.
- Buyer purchase API and a shop-operated affiliate program: unconfirmed. No public contract was found.
- Integration questions: Who fulfills each product? Which checkout warranty governs? Is an authorized reseller or purchase API available?

## FastAlts

FastAlts at `fastalts.com` is distinct from the existing Alts.fast entry at `alts.fast`.

### Catalog and pricing

- [Account catalog](https://fastalts.com/minecraft/account): full-access accounts and cape-specific inventory pools.
- [Standard full-access account](https://fastalts.com/minecraft/account/full-access-account-fastalts-vUokK5): Java + Bedrock with email inbox access.
- That product's public structured data specifies **$18.99 USD**. The fetched page displayed **€16.99** as a local price.
- [Migrator account](https://fastalts.com/minecraft/account/migrator-cape-full-access-account-fastalts-RVDM2m): guarantees the Migrator Cape and email access.
- Other published pools include Vanilla, Followers, Purple Heart, Pan, Migrator + Vanilla, and two-or-more capes.

Only explicitly stated traits are guaranteed. Generic pool purchases do not guarantee every cape, rank, or name-change option shown in inventory statistics.
The directory does not repeat changing stock counts, pool probabilities, seller deposits, or the site's aggregate sales claims.

### Payments, delivery, and warranty

The [homepage FAQ](https://fastalts.com/) lists Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.
The product page describes automatic delivery to the marketplace purchase area after payment clears.

The [terms](https://fastalts.com/terms) describe escrow and release after confirmation or the order's confirmation window.
The [refund policy](https://fastalts.com/refund-policy) provides a 30-day replacement guarantee for defects present at delivery.
The first remedy is replacement. If no valid replacement exists, the policy provides a refund to the original payment method.
Buyer-caused problems and a change of mind are excluded. Report problems from the order before confirming receipt.

### Links and open questions

- Website: [fastalts.com](https://fastalts.com/).
- Support: [support page](https://fastalts.com/support) and [support@fastalts.com](mailto:support@fastalts.com), published in the homepage's organization data.
- Reviews: [FastAlts on Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/fastalts.com). Older reviews describe a generator business, so continuity with today's marketplace is unconfirmed.
- Omitted Discord: `https://discord.gg/VTnHmZGke2`. The public invite response identified the guild as **Mr Joe's server**, with no icon.
- Logo source: [official SVG icon](https://fastalts.com/icon.svg), saved unchanged as `public/accounts/fastalts.svg`.
- Start date: omitted. Current policies and old generator reviews do not establish the marketplace's launch date.
- Buyer purchase API, affiliate program, and independently verified fulfillment history: unconfirmed.
- Integration questions: Can the provider confirm its support server and business continuity? Does it offer documented buyer API access or referral attribution?

## Eldorado

### Catalog and order flow

- [Minecraft account category](https://www.eldorado.gg/minecraft-accounts/a/61-1-0): Microsoft Java/Bedrock accounts, Hypixel ranks, Bedwars and Skyblock profiles, capes, and temporary offers.
- Some results are cape codes or Game Pass access. A category minimum therefore does not establish a permanent full-access account price.
- Delivery can be automatic or manual. Account access, server status, and delivery times depend on the seller's offer.
- [TradeShield buyer protection](https://support.eldorado.gg/en/articles/8408994-tradeshield-protection-for-buyers) holds payment through the protection period.

The TradeShield article gives buyers three days to inspect delivered goods before automatic receipt confirmation.
Standard account protection then lasts five days after the order is marked received.
The [extended warranty article](https://support.eldorado.gg/en/articles/8408988-extended-account-warranty) offers paid three- or six-month extensions on eligible orders.
Approved claims refund the account price, subject to the policy. Buyer-caused bans and resale are excluded.

### Payments and contacts

The [payment guide](https://support.eldorado.gg/en/articles/10213898-payment-methods) lists cards, cryptocurrency, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.
Regional options include PayPal, Klarna, BLIK, Cash App, Alipay, PIX, iDEAL, TWINT, MobilePay, and GCash.
Availability depends on the buyer's country, currency, account, and device.
The [fees page](https://support.eldorado.gg/en/articles/8409025-seller-fees) publishes a buyer payment fee of **8% + $0.30**.

- Website: [eldorado.gg](https://www.eldorado.gg/).
- Support: [help center](https://support.eldorado.gg/) and [contact page](https://www.eldorado.gg/contact-us).
- Official footer links: [X](https://x.com/EldoradoGg), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Eldorado.gg_Official), [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@eldoradogg1), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/eldoradogg_official/), and [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/eldoradogg.official/).
- Reviews: [Eldorado on Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/eldorado.gg).
- Start year: **2018**, stated in the provider's [careers material](https://careers.eldorado.gg/jobs/8394692-chief-financial-officer).
- Logo source: [official 192-pixel favicon](https://assetsdelivery.eldorado.gg/v7/_assets_/favicons/v4/efav-192.png), saved unchanged as `public/accounts/eldorado.png`.

### API and affiliate evidence

The [seller API guide](https://support.eldorado.gg/en/articles/16127288-how-the-seller-api-works) documents offer management, seller orders, delivery, and invoices.
Verified sellers can generate credentials and exchange them for 15-minute access tokens. No test environment is documented.
This is seller automation, not evidence of a buyer purchase API. API support is listed as `api@eldorado.gg`.

The [affiliate program](https://affiliates.eldorado.gg/) advertises a 30-day tracking window, weekly requested payouts, and a $50 payout minimum.
It advertises commissions up to 50%, but the public page does not clearly establish the calculation base for that percentage.
The [affiliate terms](https://affiliates.eldorado.gg/terms-of-service) leave rates and qualifying transactions to the program or dashboard.
They require approval of promotional channels. Deal comparison and coupon sites require prior written approval.
That restriction matters for Soulfire's directory before affiliate enrollment.

Buyer API access and affiliate credit on API purchases remain unconfirmed.

## G2G

### Catalog and buyer protection

The [Minecraft category](https://www.g2g.com/categories/minecraft-account) includes full-access Microsoft accounts, Java + Bedrock, Hypixel ranks, capes, and server profiles.
It also includes low-price products described as TLauncher or non-Microsoft accounts. These are not equivalent to licensed Microsoft accounts for online authentication.
The directory does not use the category's structured-data minimum as its full-access price.

The [account insurance article](https://help.g2g.com/en/articles/10000077-how-long-is-the-insurance-period-for-game-accounts-sold-on-g2g) defines a 14-day GamerProtect period from delivery.
Eligible cases can receive a refund or replacement. Buyers must report problems with evidence and avoid confirming incorrect deliveries.
G2G states that it cannot mediate account problems after that period. A seller's separate guarantee does not extend GamerProtect.

### Payments and contacts

The [payment guide](https://www.g2g.com/payment-guide/) lists card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Skrill, Neteller, Payoneer, cryptocurrency, and regional banking options.
Fees vary by method and currency. The guide publishes separate fees for cards, wallets, and cryptocurrency.
Neither payment methods nor processing fees are universal across regions.

- Website: [g2g.com](https://www.g2g.com/).
- Support: [G2G support center](https://support.g2g.com/en/support/home).
- Official footer links: [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/g2g_global/), [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/G2Gdotcom), and [X](https://x.com/G2Gdotcom).
- Reviews: [G2G on Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.g2g.com).
- Start year: **2013**, published in the homepage's organization structured data.
- Logo source: [official 128-pixel favicon](https://www.g2g.com/marketplace-ssr/icons/favicon-128x128.png), saved unchanged as `public/accounts/g2g.png`.

### API and affiliate evidence

The [API requirements](https://support.g2g.com/support/solutions/articles/5000880215-integrating-with-g2g-s-api-requirements-and-process) describe approved business sellers in Malaysia and Indonesia.
The published categories are gift cards, video games, and software. That page does not establish buyer API purchases of Minecraft accounts.
It links to [G2G OpenAPI](https://docs.g2g.com/) and the [official sample repository](https://github.com/g2g-official/open-api-sample).

The [affiliate FAQ](https://help.g2g.com/en/articles/10000170-affiliate-program-faq-earn-money-with-g2g-reflinks-updated-2025) describes **20% of G2G's net profit**, not 20% of the order value.
Only new users qualify. Their later purchases can also generate commission.
Enrollment requires eKYC. The [program entry point](https://www.g2g.com/affiliate) requires JavaScript.
Buyer purchase API access and API affiliate attribution remain unconfirmed.

## GameBoost

### Catalog, delivery, and warranty

The [Minecraft category](https://gameboost.com/minecraft/accounts) includes permanent accounts, Hypixel ranks, Bedwars and Skyblock profiles, capes, and temporary Game Pass products.
Some results are cape codes or names rather than full accounts.
The [sample permanent Java + Bedrock offer](https://gameboost.com/minecraft/accounts/minecraft-java-bedrock-pcxbox-full-access-ready-to-play) advertised **€8.90** and automatic delivery.
It stated that email and password could change. Other offers show manual delivery estimates.
The directory does not convert that sample into an unsupported USD starting price.

The [terms](https://gameboost.com/legal/terms) provide a free 14-day account warranty and an optional paid lifetime warranty.
The [refund policy](https://help.gameboost.com/articles/0203124-gameboost-refund-and-warranty-policy) describes seller contact followed by support review.
Depending on the case, resolution can include replacement, recovery, store credit, or a refund.
Original-method refunds require support and can take seven days. Changes of mind after delivery are excluded.

The category footer displays cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna, SEPA, Paysafecard, cryptocurrency, Skrill, and Neteller.
Checkout availability and fees still require an order-specific check.

An [indexed legacy seller API guide](https://help-beta.gameboost.com/articles/2623878-how-do-i-manage-my-listings-on-gameboost) describes seller inventory and order management.
The legacy hostname failed DNS resolution during direct verification. This source cannot establish a current buyer API contract.

Third-party affiliate directories mention GameBoost, but no applicable first-party public affiliate terms were confirmed.
A similarly named affiliate page describing subscriptions does not establish terms for this account marketplace.
Buyer purchase API access, affiliate enrollment terms, and API attribution remain open questions.

Direct requests to GameBoost returned HTTP 403. Its catalog, sample product, terms, and policy content were available through web indexing.
Official social links were verified from the provider's footer. Some social platforms blocked automated destination fetching.
Those links are first-party-published destinations, not claims that every external profile was independently inspected.

## Maintenance checks

Before changing a price, compare the exact account type and currency with the linked offer.
Before adding a warranty badge, read the product exclusions and distinguish included coverage from paid upgrades.
Before adding Discord, verify the invite's guild identity and expiry through Discord's public invite endpoint.
Before adding affiliate attribution, obtain approved Soulfire links and confirm the directory as an accepted promotional channel.
Before implementing purchases, obtain current buyer API documentation, delivery formats, retry behavior, and explicit affiliate attribution rules.
