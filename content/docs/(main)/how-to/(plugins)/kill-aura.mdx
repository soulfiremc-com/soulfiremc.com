## What this is for

**Auto attack** makes a bot look at and attack the closest target in range.
Use it for combat tests, PvP experiments, and entity targeting under load.
Releases up to 2.10.7 call this feature **Kill Aura** or KillAura. The plugin ID stays `kill-aura`.

A target is any attackable entity, including mobs and players.
The plugin skips players in creative or spectator mode, players in `whitelisted-users`, and bots of the same project.
It controls only looking, swinging, and attacking. It does not move the bot.

<Callout type="warn">
  Set `whitelisted-users` before you test near real players.
  Every other player in range is a target.
</Callout>

## Settings

| Setting | Default | What it controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `whitelisted-users` | Empty | Player names that the bot never attacks. Case does not matter. |
| `look-range` | 4.8 | Distance at which the bot turns toward a target |
| `swing-range` | 3.5 | Distance at which the bot swings its arm. `0` turns off swings without a hit. |
| `hit-range` | 3.0 | Distance at which the bot attacks |
| `check-walls` | On | Picks only targets that have a visible point |
| `ignore-cooldown` | Off | Ignores the attack cooldown of Minecraft 1.9 and later |
| `attack-delay-ticks` | 8 to 12 | Random delay between attacks, used only on 1.8 and older or with `ignore-cooldown` on |

Keep `look-range` the largest and `hit-range` the smallest.

`hit-range` cannot extend the bot's reach.
Vanilla reach and crosshair targeting still limit each attack, and SoulFire caps the range at 2.65 blocks while the bot moves.

`check-walls` affects target selection only.
With it off, the bot can turn toward a target behind a wall, but it still cannot hit through the wall.

## Enable Auto attack

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Enable the plugin

    Open **Plugins** and enable **Auto attack**.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Protect players

    Add every player name that the bot must not attack to `whitelisted-users`.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Test with one bot

    Keep the default ranges and `check-walls` on.
    Check targeting and attack timing with one bot.
    Then change one setting at a time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common failure patterns

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| The bot looks at targets but does not hit | Increase `hit-range`, or wait for the attack cooldown. A moving bot hits only within 2.65 blocks. |
| The bot swings before it can hit | Decrease `swing-range` |
| The bot turns toward targets that it cannot see | Turn on `check-walls`, then decrease `look-range` |
| The bot attacks a protected player | Check the spelling in `whitelisted-users` |
| The bot ignores a player | The player is in creative or spectator mode, is whitelisted, or is a bot of the same project |
