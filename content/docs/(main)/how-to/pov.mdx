A bot view helps you inspect the world around a connected bot.
Use it after a connection succeeds, rather than as a test of authentication.

## Inspect one bot

1. Open the instance's **Overview**.
2. Select a connected bot and open its detail view.
3. Open its point-of-view controls.
4. Compare the view with a normal player at the same location.

Check the position, dimension, loaded chunks, and nearby objects.
A bot can be connected while the world has not loaded enough for the action you want.

## Capture an image

The terminal provides camera exports for selected bots:

```text
bot DocsBot_1 export-basic-render 640 360
```

Use `export-render-debug-dump` to collect camera and device details for a rendering problem.
Read [commands](/docs/reference/commands) for supported arguments.
The backend writes exports to its data directories, not the remote client's computer.

## Automate camera inspection

Use [SDK camera and maps](/docs/sdk/camera) for captures, streams, and world maps.
A screenshot is useful evidence for a failed interaction. Also record the bot position, connection state, and action error.
